VIDEO BACKGROUND - drop-in folder
==================================

The hero looks finished with NO video (a warm forest/gold gradient + film grain
fallback renders automatically). To add a cinematic video background:

1. Add your files here:
     hero.mp4    (H.264, 1080p, ~6-8 MB, muted, 10-20s seamless loop)
     hero.webm   (VP9 - smaller; optional but recommended)

2. Add a poster still to ../img/ :
     hero-poster.jpg   (a strong frame from the video; shown before it plays
                        and on mobile, where the video is skipped to save data)

3. In index.html, find "VIDEO BACKGROUND SLOT" in the hero and UNCOMMENT the
   <video> ... </video> block.

Suggested royalty-free sources & search terms:
  - Pexels Videos  (pexels.com/videos)
  - Coverr         (coverr.co)
  - Mixkit         (mixkit.co/free-stock-video)
  Search: "Washington DC golden hour skyline", "classic apartment building
  facade", "city aerial dusk", "architecture sunset".

Tip: keep it slow, warm, and architectural - it should sit BEHIND the headline,
not compete with it. The dark scrim over it keeps the text readable.
